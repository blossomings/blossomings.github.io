Simple Spanish Syllables | T, N, D

Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to
➧ Recognize the letters T, N, and D.
➧ Pronounce each letter correctly.
➧ Blend these consonants with the five Spanish vowels.
➧ Read simple Spanish syllables.
➧ Read common Spanish words.
➧ Read short Spanish sentences.
➧ Improve your reading confidence.
