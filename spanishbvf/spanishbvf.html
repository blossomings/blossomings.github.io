Introduction

Today you'll learn three new letters





These letters are very common in Spanish.
You'll also learn one of the most important pronunciation rules
In most varieties of Spanish, the letters B and V are pronounced the same.
Unlike English, native Spanish speakers usually do not make a different sound for B and V.
For example





Both begin with almost the same sound.

Letter Name | be
Some regions call it be alta or be larga to distinguish it from V.

Sound | /b/
At the beginning of a sentence or after m or n, it sounds similar to the English b.
Between vowels, the sound becomes softer.
Don't worry about mastering the softer sound yet. Focus on reading correctly.
