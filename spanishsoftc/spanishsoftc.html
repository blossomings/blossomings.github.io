The Letter C | Soft Sound - CE, CI

Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to
➧ Recognize the letter C before E and I.
➧ Pronounce CE and CI correctly.
➧ Understand the difference between CA, CO, CU and CE, CI.
➧ Read simple words containing CE and CI.
➧ Understand the difference between Latin American and Spain pronunciation.
➧ Read short sentences using the soft C sound.
